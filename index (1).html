02 / STRUCTURAL DESIGN + FEA
EMAE 260 · Team project · 2025

Modular Vertical Farming System

Developed a concept for a space-efficient vertical farm on a campus parking-garage wall. The final design measured 6 ft 8 in wide × 8 ft tall × 2 ft deep, with five removable troughs for 25 plants, a galvanized frame, polycarbonate weather protection, sliding doors, and adjustable drip irrigation. Contributed SolidWorks frame modeling, structural FEA for plant/soil/snow loads, and BOM/design trade-offs.

SolidWorksFEAStructural steelBOM25-plant design capacity
Engineering approach + contribution

The final design specification describes bolt-together framing, removable soil bags, and modular planting. Team-level work included requirements development, growing-system research, material choices, maintainability, and environmental loads. This was a proof-of-concept design; the eight-year life and crop targets are design requirements, not verified field performance.
